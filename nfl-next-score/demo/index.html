How does this work?

The model is called XGBoost. It's made of 1,936 small yes/no "decision trees". Each tree asks questions like "Is the ball inside the opponent's 30?" or "Are there less than 2 minutes left?" and then gives a small vote for one of the 11 possible outcomes. The page adds up all the votes and turns them into percentages that add up to 100%.

It was trained on every play from the 2018–2024 NFL seasons and tested on the 2025 season, which it had never seen. On 2025, its top guess was exactly right 38% of the time. Simple guessing was right 28% of the time. When it says something has about a 30% chance, that thing happened about 30% of the time.

"Return TD" means any touchdown that isn't a normal run or catch: kick or punt returns, interception returns, fumble returns, and so on. The model does not know which teams or players are playing.
