Same Algorithm, More Data

100 copies of one NFL prediction algorithm. Predictor 1 learns from 0 games, predictor 100 learns from games, and the rest learn from amounts in between. Here is what each one picks.

Every predictor looks at the same five things about both teams: this season's record, the average point margin over its last 3 games, how high it picked in this year's draft, players gained minus players lost in trades this year, and its roster: how good the players who played in its last game are, based on how their teams did in each player's last 17 games. From the games it learns from, it works out how much each one matters. The predictors learn only from games since the 2020 season. The trade list only goes up to April 2026, so trades made since then are not counted.

Each predictor learns from the most recent finished games. Change a matchup, remove it, or add your own. Tap a square to see that predictor's pick.
